Where the serif travels

Instrument Serif is made for large sizes and has one weight. It carries the app's voice only where text is big, short and not interactive. Everything that is read in a line, tapped, or full of figures stays in the sans.

Serif20 sp and up: the headline sentence, screen titles, month names, empty-state headline, dialog titles, onboarding headlines.
SansAnything under 20 sp, every button and chip, every paragraph, every date and count (Instrument Serif's flagless 1 reads as a lowercase L).
NeverSerif on a tappable thing, or serif below 20 sp.
