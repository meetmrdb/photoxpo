# Photoxpo privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Photoxpo backs up the photos and videos on your phone to a USB drive you choose. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## What Photoxpo collects

Nothing. Photoxpo has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting, and it does not have permission to use the internet. No data about you or your photos is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What stays on your phone and your drive

To do its job, Photoxpo reads the photos and videos in your phone's camera folder and:

- copies each one, at full quality, to the folder you picked on your USB drive;
- keeps a small Preview of each photo on your phone so you can browse them while the drive is unplugged;
- keeps a record on your phone of which photos and videos it has backed up and checked;
- when you tap Load, copies that one photo or video from your drive back into a "Photoxpo Loaded" folder on your phone.

The photos' dates and location data (if they have any) are copied with them, so your library keeps its dates and places. Photoxpo uses them only to show your photos in the right order.

All of this stays on your phone and your drive. Uninstalling Photoxpo deletes the Previews and the record from your phone. The copies on your drive stay there, because they are yours.

## Permissions

- **Photos and videos:** to read the photos and videos it backs up.
- **Photo location:** so the location saved in your photos is copied with them.
- **Notifications:** to show a Backup run's progress while it runs.
- **Run in the background and ignore battery optimisation:** so a long Backup run keeps going with the screen off.
- **Your USB drive:** you pick a folder on the drive yourself, and Photoxpo can only reach that folder.

## Children

Photoxpo is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
